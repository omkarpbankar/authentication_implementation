# Your Authentication Journey

This course assumes you are new to authentication. Each phase explains the ideas in plain English, has you build or try something, then checks your understanding with a small test.

## How to use this course

1. Open the lesson for your current phase.
2. Read one section at a time, and run the hands-on steps yourself.
3. Write what you learned in your own words in `docs/`. Explaining an idea is the best proof you understand it.
4. Take the small test without looking at the answers.
5. Reach the pass mark before moving on, because each phase builds on the one before it.

**Want help?** Ask Claude to teach a phase or quiz you, for example "teach me Phase 1" or "test me on Phase 1". Claude asks one question at a time, checks your reasoning, and updates your score below. If you get stuck, say so and ask for a different explanation.

New words are defined in the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md).

## The story of the course

- **Phases 0 and 1:** Set up your tools, and learn how login works at the most basic level.
- **Phases 2 to 5:** Learn tokens, then let trusted providers handle login for you.
- **Phases 6 and 7:** Connect outside providers, and let one login open many apps.
- **Phases 8 and 9:** Keep customers' data apart, and control what each person can do.
- **Phases 10 to 12:** Add stronger login, control access to whole apps, and prepare for real use.

Every phase is built. Each lesson has a **Run it** section with exact commands, and [the checklist](CHECKLIST.md) shows how each phase was verified and which steps need your own accounts.
